I am a Computer Science PhD student and Graduate Research Assistant at The University of Texas at Arlington, advised by Dr. Mohammad Atiqul Islam. I work at the intersection of Agentic AI, NLP, large language models, trustworthy AI, and efficient LLM systems. Before my PhD, I spent more than three years as a Machine Learning Engineer at IQVIA, building production ML and LLM systems. My work spans top-tier research, including an ICML Spotlight and a first-author EACL Findings paper, as well as deployed AI systems whose insights contributed to a 15% increase in market sales.
